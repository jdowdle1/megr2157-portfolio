## Objective
My objective is to create a motor mount using a loaf and material characteristics. To determine which has a bigger effect on the mount, I'll utilize stress and deflection. The thickness of the mount will be determined by the greater of the two forces. I choose the width and length at my own discretion. The motor mount will be fixed to a surface on one side (Feature 2). The mount's motor side must meet the requirements of the particular stepper motor (Feature 1).

## Analyze
In order to comprehend the features' dimensions and how the motor should attach to features 1 and 2, I made free body diagrams of each feature.

## Decide
I determined the moment generated on the mount and used the provided formulas to solve for the motor mount's thickness on each feature independently using the maximum stress, modulus, and safety factor. on both aspects. More thickness was needed for the deflection than for the stress. To achieve the strongest mount possible, I went with the higher value.
