#About Me
# Jackson Dowdle MEGR 2157 – Design Portfolio 
I am a mechanical engineering student at the University of North Carolina at Charlotte with a strong interest in understanding how mechanical systems work and how engineering principles can be applied to solve practical problems. My education through McDowell Early College and McDowell Technical Community College gave me a foundation in engineering, science, mathematics, and hands-on technical skills. My experience with engineering coursework and mechatronics has strengthened my interest in designing, analyzing, and improving mechanical systems.

What brought me to mechanical engineering is my interest in understanding how physical systems operate and how individual components work together to accomplish a specific function. I enjoy working through problems that require both mathematical analysis and practical reasoning. Hands-on experiences have also shown me the importance of understanding the connection between theoretical equations and the physical behavior of a system. Mechanical engineering appeals to me because it combines mathematics, physics, design, problem-solving, and practical application.

As I continue developing as an engineer, I am becoming someone who focuses on understanding the reasoning behind a design rather than simply finding an answer that works. I want to develop the ability to analyze a problem, identify the important variables and constraints, evaluate possible solutions, and justify why one solution is better than another. My goal is to become an engineer who can work effectively in both technical and practical environments and communicate engineering decisions clearly to others.

I recognize that becoming a successful engineer requires more than technical knowledge. It also requires attention to detail, professional communication, teamwork, adaptability, and the ability to learn from mistakes. Through my education and future engineering experiences, I intend to continue developing these skills while building a strong foundation in mechanical engineering
